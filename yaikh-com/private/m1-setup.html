The department PA collective

Big Brain isn't a monolith — it's the 3 management modules (Dashboard · Data Scientist · SOP) sitting on top of the department PAs. 13 core PAs, plus Social as the 14th. Each PA owns its patch, alerts its department head, escalates to Big Brain when silence itself becomes the event.

Git & deploy pipeline

A source-file edit in yaikh-dashboard/src/ is invisible until the CRA is rebuilt and its output committed. This is the trap that ate 7 pushes in a row.

Who owns what · session coordination

Five Claude sessions divide the work by machine and by scope. Push protocol: whoever owns the code edits and commits; the current front-man session (name shifts after restart — was gk-dev-7e, currently gk-dev-05) pushes.
