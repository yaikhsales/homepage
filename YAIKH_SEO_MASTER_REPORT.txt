1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Yaikh (yaikh.com) is an AI-powered enterprise management platform built by
TexLink Technologies Co., Ltd., headquartered in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

It serves as an all-in-one smart factory and business management system,
combining traditional ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) features with
cutting-edge AI agents, IoT (Internet of Things) sensor integration, and
digital transformation tools.

The demo dashboard is accessible at: https://demo1.yaikh.com
The live production system runs at: https://ym.yaikh.com

Yaikh is primarily designed for garment factories, manufacturing companies,
and industrial enterprises in Southeast Asia — especially Cambodia — that
want to digitize their operations, automate HR, track energy and resources,
manage quality control, and deploy AI assistants for every department.

3. WHAT YAIKH DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE FOR SEARCH ENGINES)
================================================================================

Yaikh is a comprehensive AI-powered enterprise resource planning (ERP) and
smart factory management platform that helps manufacturing companies digitize
and automate every aspect of their business operations.

IN SIMPLE TERMS, YAIKH HELPS BUSINESSES:

  ✓ Manage their entire human resources (HR) pipeline — from recruitment,
    interviewing, onboarding, attendance tracking, payroll, NSSF compliance,
    visa/work permits, to canteen management.

  ✓ Track factory production in real-time — monitoring work-in-progress (WIP),
    production line performance, machine maintenance, and output quality.

  ✓ Control quality at every stage — from fabric receiving, cutting inspection,
    sewing QC, finishing, packing, to final buyer inspection using the YQMS
    (Yaikh Quality Management System).

  ✓ Monitor energy, water, air quality, and waste using IoT sensors — helping
    factories reduce consumption, comply with environmental standards, and
    achieve sustainability goals.

  ✓ Automate financial workflows — purchase requests, approvals, payments,
    invoicing, tax reporting, and trial balance reports.

  ✓ Digitize audits — digital compliance certificates, 6S checklists, audit
    plans, and corrective action plans (CAP).

  ✓ Deploy AI assistants for every department — AI bots for Finance (Finance PA),
    HR (HR PA), Admin (Admin PA), Quality (QMS PA), Production (Production PA),
    CSR (CSR PA), Sales (Sale PA), and Social Media (Social PA).

  ✓ Connect to Cambodian government e-services — CCF, MISTI, OWSO, E-Filing,
    and NSSF portals directly from the dashboard.

4.1 ADMINISTRATION SECTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- ACCOUNTANT MODULE ---
  The Accountant module is Yaikh's complete financial management system.
  It handles the full cycle of purchase requests and financial reporting.

  Sub-modules:
  • Verify PR (Purchase Request Verification)
    - Finance teams use this to review and verify incoming purchase requests.
    - Can check request details, attached invoices, and supporting documents.
    - Ensures accuracy before approval.

  • Approval PR (Purchase Request Approval)
    - Senior managers or department heads approve or reject verified PRs.
    - Full status tracking shows pending, approved, or rejected states.
    - Audit trail maintained for compliance.

  • Pay PR (Purchase Request Payment)
    - Processes actual payments for approved purchase requests.
    - Links payment records to original purchase requests.
    - Maintains a complete financial audit trail.

  • TB Monthly Yearly (Trial Balance)
    - Generates Trial Balance financial reports for monthly and yearly periods.
    - Helps accountants reconcile accounts and prepare financial statements.
    - Connected to the ym.yaikh.com production accounting system.

  • TOI (Transfer of Information)
    - Global data transfer and information-sharing tool.
    - Connects factory data across departments and external systems.

  • Factory Accounting
    - Handles factory-level accounting operations and cost calculations.
    - Tracks production costs, overheads, and departmental budgets.

  • TAX Reporting
    - Generates tax reports and compliance documentation.
    - Supports Cambodian tax filing requirements.

--- E-INVOICING MODULE ---
  Digital invoicing system with three components:
  • Cambodia E-Invoice — Compliant with Cambodian government e-invoice standards.
  • Supplier Management — Manages supplier profiles, contacts, and payment terms.
  • IEWS (Integrated E-commerce Workflow System) — Connects invoicing to orders.
