Step one is a contract, not a phone call

Yorkwell’s development agreement decides whether this arm exists. Get it to Koem Phanny with one question: may Texlink sell Yai to a factory outside the Yorkwell group?

Do not sound out a single vendor group before that answer. If a competitor hears it and mentions it back to Yorkwell, the relationship that funds the build is the thing at risk.

The door

Yorkwell’s group IT or operations head in Hong Kong. One person who can name their counterpart at all nine groups above. These groups are not strangers — they share buyers, sit in the same vendor summits and benchmark against each other constantly.

The bridge

Frame it as their win. A vendor group running Yai looks better to its brands, and Yorkwell introducing it to a peer is Yorkwell leading the vendor community. A referral fee or reseller margin makes it their revenue too.

That framing survives an exclusivity clause far better than “let us sell to your competitors” does.

The route that bypasses the contract

Track people, not companies. Plant managers, IE heads and compliance managers move between these groups constantly, and someone who ran Yai at Yorkmars asks for it at the next job. Nothing in any contract stops us staying in touch with alumni.

Never HQ first — the trading office first

Those offices already audit our plants. They know Yorkmars by name. They have never heard of Yai. That gap is the whole opening, and it sits with people who already have us in their calendar.

The warm human we already own

Yorkmars’ own compliance manager emails these brand auditors every week. Ask for the names of the ASICS and VF auditors who physically visit — a real relationship, held by our own customer, costing nothing.

Then invite the auditor to the plant to watch the video feed run. Demo on the floor, never in a deck.

Triangulating the ASICS lead

No name is published. Three ways to it, run in parallel:

(a) the governance section of the 2025 Sustainability Report lists committee members by role.

(b) LinkedIn — ASICS + sustainability + Kobe, then check who our Yorkmars and Caswell compliance staff are connected to at one remove.

(c) Cascale, Textile Exchange and SLCP working groups publish attendee and member lists, and brand sustainability staff sit on them under their own names.

Buy a seat at the standards bodies

Associate or solution-provider membership of Cascale or amfori is a paid listing and, more importantly, a speaking slot — where brand sustainability staff actually gather. CDMS already implements all 36 of these standards, so the credential is real before we pay for the seat.

What we open with

ASICS publishes “100% supplier compliance with ASICS standards.” The question is not a pitch — it is “how is that evidenced today?” Then show a Khmer safety notice with a completion list against it.

Brands pay auditors for weaker evidence than that.
