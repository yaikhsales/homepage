## 01 · Executive Summary

> **Factory-tested for 5 years inside live production facilities — Ai MIP is opening its gates to the industry for the first time.**

**What it is.** Yai is **Ai MIP** — Agentic Manufacturing Intelligence. A three-layer platform that modernises your production unit from a whole-paper-based operation into executive Ai. The chaos most factories live in today — *paper reports and ledger books, scattered chat apps, manual signatures, staff running floor-to-floor chasing approvals, calls and pushes by chat* — is what Yai replaces.

The three layers Yai delivers, stacked on top:

1. **Digitalization layer** *(centralised data).* Excel dashboards and digital records flow into one database. Barcode & QR scanners, AIoT sensors, mobile apps and tablets — initial workflow streamlining, one source of truth.
2. **Agentic layer** *(LLM-powered intelligent agents).* Voice-to-workflow processing, text instructions interpreted by LLM, geolocation & logistics optimisation, intuitive dashboards and DTV (Digital Twin Visualisation), real-time Ai guidance for staff — agents refining workflows.
3. **Full Ai layer** *(strategic management & growth).* Higher-level management decision-making, strategic planning with Ai insights, predictive business growth, multi-factory management, business expansion and global growth.

## 06 · The Agents & Their Skills

> **The full platform, laid out exactly like the live dashboard — Administration, Management, Operations. Every module is an Ai agent. Tap one to hear what it does.**

### Administration Band

#### Accountant
- **Accountant** — I run the whole accounting function — income, expenses, invoicing, taxes and the company's books — and keep every ledger reconciled in real time.
- **IEWS** — I handle e-invoicing — issuing and validating invoices through CamInv / the GDT e-invoice system so every invoice is compliant and traceable.

#### Billing
- **Purchase Request** — I handle purchase requests — from raising to approval routing and PO creation.
- **Bill Claim** — I process expense and bill claims — submission, approval and reimbursement straight back to the claimant.
- **Salary Bill** — I run payroll end to end — wages, overtime and deductions — then pay everyone out straight to their ABA or WING account. Each person sees every payment and payslip detail right inside the Yai app.

#### HR
- **YHR** — I'm the HR core — employee records, contracts, leave and the full worker lifecycle in one place.
- **Org Chart** — I keep the company's structure live — every role, reporting line and headcount. The chart updates itself the moment someone joins or resigns.
- **Training** — I plan and track training — schedules, attendance and the skills record for every worker.
- **Temporary Worker** — I manage temporary-worker requests — raising, approving and tracking short-term labour on the floor.
- **Speak Up** — I'm the anonymous grievance channel — workers raise concerns safely and I route them to the right people.

#### Admin
- **Support Ticket** — I take internal support requests — log, assign and track every ticket to resolution.
- **Y Shop** — I run the internal shop — staff purchases, stock and orders.
- **Gate Pass** — I issue and verify gate passes — people and goods in and out, logged and authorised.
- **Meeting Room** — I manage meeting-room bookings — availability, scheduling and conflicts.
- **Car Booking** — I handle company-car bookings — requests, allocation and the schedule.
- **Fire Alarm** — I monitor the fire-alarm and life-safety systems across the whole site.
- **CCTV** — I connect to the CCTV system — live and recorded camera access for security.

#### CSR
- **Digital Audit** — I run digital compliance audits — WRAP, BSCI, HIGG and ILO evidence collected and scored automatically.
- **Energy** — I track energy use — kWh, efficiency and renewable share across the factory.
- **Air** — I monitor air emissions — AQI, CO2 and air-quality data for compliance.
- **Water** — I monitor water — usage and effluent/wastewater levels for environmental reporting.
- **Waste** — I track waste — solid waste, recycling rates and disposal records.
- **Chemical** — I manage chemical inventory and MRSL / ZDHC compliance.

#### Shipping
- **Shipping** — I handle freight both ways — importing raw materials and machines, and exporting finished goods by FCL and LCL — tracking every container, customs step and delivery end to end.

#### E-Gov
- **E-Government** — I connect directly to every government portal — the Tax portal, the Customs portal, and each ministry's compliance portal. I also run the communication channel with each government body, handling official PR and correspondence.

### Management Band

#### Dashboard
- **Management Dashboard** — I'm the GM's data keeper. Every number in the company runs through me — production, sales, finance, HR, compliance — so the General Manager's single screen knows literally everything, live.
- **SOP** — I'm the process police. Every standard operating procedure lives with me — searchable, versioned and always current — and nobody here does anything without going through my process first.

#### Noticeable
- **System Analysis** — Cross-functional system-wide analytics and reporting.

### Operations Band

#### QA
- **YQMS** — I'm quality management — inline and final QC, defects, AQL and corrective actions.
- **Call Out** — I escalate quality issues — flagging defects and calling out the right people the moment something needs attention.

#### Production
- **FC** — I run floor control — line balancing, output and live production status.
- **YWIP** — I'm all about quantities — from your fabric rolls, to the cut panels, to the finished goods, to the cartons ready to export.
- **CE** — I handle costing & efficiency — SAM, line efficiency and cost per garment.
- **YTM** — I manage time & motion — SAM studies, method and capacity planning.
- **YTM Shop** — I'm the shop-floor time view — operation breakdowns right at the station.

#### 4DP
- **4DP** — I'm the production plan — scheduling orders across lines day by day.

#### YPi
- **YPI** — I deliver garment technical specs in Khmer — construction, measurements and machine settings on the floor.

In 90% of Cambodian factory sales conversations, the decision is **"keep using paper / spreadsheets / 10-year-old legacy"** vs **"try something new"** — not SAP vs Yai. Odoo, ERPNext. *Weakness vs Yai:* requires heavy customisation; no apparel modules out of the box; no Ai-native architecture; the integration tax falls on the factory.

Various Chinese ERP/MES. *Weakness vs Yai:* UI in Chinese only; no Khmer; no Cambodia compliance; weak service in-country.

### Tier 5 · Garment-floor point tools
Tukatech, GerberCAD, AccuMark, Wonderchain. *Weakness vs Yai:* single-purpose; don't talk to each other; the integration burden is the factory's problem.

**Paper, Excel, WhatsApp, 10-year-old legacy.** The real opponent in 90% of sales conversations.
